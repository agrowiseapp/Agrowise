Specification

Two steps

Tap the card — a sheet slides up with a short explanation and the two store buttons. Tap outside it or "Κλείσιμο" to dismiss.

Tap iOS or Android — the phone's own share sheet opens with the message below, carrying that platform's store link. The user picks WhatsApp, Viber, SMS or mail and sends it on. It never opens the store — whoever taps already has the app.

The message they send
